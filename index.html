Initiating coverage on India's nuclear energy value chain

Treasury Equity Intern · IDBI Treasury, Mumbai · Apr to Jun 2026

The problem

The desk wanted a view on which listed names would gain from India's nuclear build-out. The information sat in company filings, regulatory orders and order books. No two companies reported it the same way.

What I built

One repeatable funnel for all fifteen companies. Each name went through the same risk, valuation and governance tests before it could get a recommendation, so the calls could be compared across the set.

The output

I wrote the sector thesis with a recommendation on each name and presented it to the treasury desk.

  4. Veto 5 conditions on governance, promoter pledge, audit quality
  5. Score 7-dimension weighted model: technology, policy, valuation, management, order book
  6. Result 4 conviction bands, one call per name
